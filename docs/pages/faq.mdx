# FAQ

## How do I choose the right plan?
If you're an individual developer or working on small projects, the Standard plan should meet your needs. For teams or businesses requiring advanced collaboration features, the Pro plan is recommended.

## Do you offer a free trial?

Yes, we offer a 14-day trial period when you subscribe to any plan. During these 14 days, you can explore all features of CelerBuild without being charged. If you're not satisfied, you can cancel anytime within the trial period at no cost. If you decide to continue, billing will start automatically after the 14-day trial ends.


## How does CelerBuild handle my data and privacy?

CelerBuild is a self-hosted solution that prioritizes your privacy:

- All your deployment data, configurations, and logs remain on your infrastructure
- We do not collect or have access to your deployment data
- The only connection to our servers is for license validation purposes
- No usage statistics or personal information is transmitted to our servers

## Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Whether you're upgrading or downgrading, once you change your subscription plan and complete the payment, you'll immediately have access to all the features of your new plan. The new billing cycle will start from the moment the change takes effect.

##  What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards and debit cards via Stripe.
