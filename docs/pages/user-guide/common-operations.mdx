# Common Operations

## User Login

1. Access the CelerBuild login page at `http://localhost:8666/login` (or your configured domain)
2. Enter your credentials:
     - Email or Username
     - Password
3. Click "Login" to access your account

<Callout type="info">
    If you forgot your password, it can only be reset by your system administrator. Once reset, you can log in and change your password by navigating to `Settings` under `Profile` and selecting `Change Password`.
</Callout>

## User Logout

To logout from CelerBuild:
1. Click your profile icon in the top-right corner
2. Select "Logout" from the dropdown menu

## Profile Settings

### Update Avatar
1. Click your profile icon
2. Select "Profile" under Settings
3. Click "Change Avatar"
4. Choose an image file (supported formats: JPG, PNG)
5. Click "Save Change" to update your avatar

### Change Password
1. Click your profile icon
2. Select "Profile" under Settings
3. Locate the "Change Password"
4. Enter your current password
5. Enter your new password
6. Confirm your new password
7. Click "Change Password" to save changes

<Callout type="info">
    Your profile information (User ID, Username, Email, and Roles) is displayed for reference but cannot be modified through the interface. For any changes to your account information, please contact your system administrator.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
    When changing your password:
    - Make sure to remember your new password
    - Use a strong password for better security
    - Do not share your password with others
</Callout>
