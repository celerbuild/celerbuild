# Developer Role in CelerBuild
import { Callout } from 'nextra-theme-docs'

As a Developer in CelerBuild, you play a crucial role in the deployment process. This guide will help you understand your role and responsibilities.

## Key Responsibilities

1. Initiating deployment requests
2. Executing approved deployment tasks
3. Initiating rollback requests
4. Executing approved rollback tasks

    - [Executing Deployment Tasks](/user-guide/execute-deployment-task)
    - [Rollback Deployment](/user-guide/rollback-deployment)

## Additional Notes for Developers

### Deployment Review Process
- Your deployment requests need approval from Project Owner or Project Master
- You'll be notified once your request is approved
- Only approved deployments can be executed

### Monitoring and Communication
- Keep track of your deployment requests in the Applications section
- Monitor deployment progress in the Tasks section
- Communicate with reviewers if changes or clarifications are needed

## Best Practices

- Always double-check your deployment settings before submitting a request
- Communicate with your team about upcoming deployments
- Monitor the deployment process and be ready to respond to any issues
- Keep your local development environment up-to-date to minimize deployment conflicts

## Need Help?

If you encounter any issues or have questions about your role as a Developer in CelerBuild, please don't hesitate to contact us or consult your project's Owner or Master.
