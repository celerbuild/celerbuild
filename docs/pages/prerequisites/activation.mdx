# Software Activation

After installing CelerBuild, you'll need to activate the software before proceeding with configuration.

## Obtaining Activation Details

1. Visit [CelerBuild Account Page](https://celerbuild.com/account)
2. Log in with your account credentials
3. Locate your activation information:
    - Activation Email
    - Activation Code

<Callout type="info">
    Make sure to use the exact email address associated with your CelerBuild account.
</Callout>

## Activating CelerBuild

1. Access the CelerBuild dashboard
```
http://<your-host>:<port>/login
   ```
<Callout type="info">
    Replace:
    - `<your-host>` with your server's hostname or IP address (e.g., localhost, 192.168.1.100)
    - `<port>` with your configured port number (default: 8666)
</Callout>

2. Log in with default credentials:
```
Username: admin
Password: admin@12345
   ```

<Callout type="warning">
    For security reasons, please change your password immediately after first login. You can change your password in Settings/Profile.
</Callout>

3. Navigate to Settings > Subscription
4. Enter your activation details:
    - Paste your Activation Email
    - Paste your Activation Code
5. Click "Activate" to complete the process


## Verifying Activation

After successful activation, you should see your subscription details:
- Email address
- Plan type:
    - Version: Standard/Pro
    - Period: Monthly/Annual
- Expiry Date (UTC)
